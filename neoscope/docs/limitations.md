# Research limitations

- Synthetic sessions are generated by an explicit simulator and are not market observations. Results on them demonstrate pipeline behavior, not investment efficacy.
- Live results depend on the selected provider's entitlements, API environment, feed coverage, market hours, and observed network health. Missing data must not be interpreted as no market activity.
- NEO and SSI quote data are aggregated displayed depth. Neither feed shows order IDs, queue priority, cancellations by participant, hidden liquidity, or the user's true executable price.
- Quote examples use thousand-VND units; trade messages use VND. The adapter's conversion is documented and raw records preserve source values. Independently spot-check units with SSI before any operational use.
- The source's displayed average price and cumulative volume yield an approximate cumulative trade value. They do not reconstruct exact tick-by-tick value.
- SSI documents foreign buy/sell quantity/value fields without specifying their counter semantics in the current contract. NeoScope preserves these source fields and excludes them from cumulative foreign-flow analytics until confirmed.
- Provider schemas differ. Unavailable or semantically unverified values remain null; analyses depending on them need source-specific validation. Sessions from different providers are isolated and must not be treated as a continuous panel without reconciliation.
- The paper matching engine uses assumptions for participation, fees, taxes, fill horizon, and terminal marking. It does not model queue priority, market impact feedback, exchange latency, or all market rules. Its output is an educational estimate, not an execution promise.
- Historical or simulated out-of-sample validation does not establish future profitability. Session clustering reduces pseudoreplication but cannot remove regime shifts, selection bias, or data vendor effects.
- This is a research and decision-support prototype. It is not a suitability assessment, investment recommendation, compliance system, or production trading platform.
